## Why choose Kindstart

- **Help arrives at the moment:** realizations appear when you postpone, miss or finish a task, at night, and after the questions. Not found in the nine apps.
- **It asks before it plans:** a few taps before each task can bring up the right message.
- **Need help, then crisis, in two taps,** with crisis lines checked against the official sites.
- **No AI, no install, no account needed,** free in any browser.
